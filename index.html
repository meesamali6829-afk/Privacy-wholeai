1. About Whole AI

Whole AI is an AI-powered coding and building workspace. It helps developers, founders and creators review code, find and fix bugs, modernize code, scan for security issues, ask coding questions, and build websites, mobile apps and full-stack projects from a phone or computer. You sign in with your Google account, and your plan, credits and saved work are stored in your account.

Whole AI is operated by the Whole AI team. If you have any question about this policy, write to wholeaisupport@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide or that comes from your Google account

  • Account information: when you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and profile photo (if available) from Google. We use these to create and identify your account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Profile data: if you upload or change a profile photo or edit your profile, we store that information in your account.
  • Content you submit: the code, prompts, messages, files and images you enter or attach to features such as Code Review, Bug Hunter, Everything AI, AI Assistant, Build Web, Build App and the Full Stack Builder.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact support.

2.2 Information created while you use the Service

  • Plan, credits and usage: your current plan (Free, Pro or Heavy Pro), credit balance, plan expiry date, daily and bonus credits, and which features you used, so we can run billing and limits correctly.
  • Saved chats and projects: your chat history, generated code and projects, so you can come back to them.
  • Published sites: if you publish a website, we store its files, its public name (the part before .wholeai.space, for example yourname.wholeai.space), and any custom domain you connect.
  • Transaction details: the plan you bought, the amount, the time and the status of the payment (see Section 5).

2.3 Technical information

  • Device identifier: a randomly generated identifier stored on your device, used for abuse prevention (see Section 9).
  • IP address: processed by our servers to deliver the Service and to prevent abuse. We store it only as described in Section 9.
  • Local data on your device: chat history, preferences and sign-in state kept in your browser or app storage (see Section 8).
We do not collect your precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, SMS, call logs or photos from your gallery unless you yourself choose to attach a file or image inside the app.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To process your requests with AI and return a response, build result or preview.
  • To manage your plan, credits, daily limits, subscription period and billing.
  • To save your chats, projects and published sites so they are available across sessions and devices.
  • To send you service emails, such as a welcome message and subscription confirmations.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, policy violations and technical problems.
  • To keep the Service secure, reliable and working properly, and to fix errors.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent it or trade it for marketing. We do not show third-party advertising in Whole AI.

4. AI processing of your content

Whole AI is powered by third-party AI models. When you use an AI feature, the content you submit (your prompt, your code, any file or image you attach, and the relevant conversation context) is sent to our AI providers, Gemini and DeepSeek, so that a response can be generated and sent back to you.

  • We send only what is needed to answer your request.
  • To test and fix code, some features run the code you submit in an isolated, secure sandbox provided by Daytona.
  • Do not submit passwords, API keys, private keys, payment card numbers, government ID numbers or other highly sensitive information in your prompts or code.
  • AI output can be incorrect, incomplete or insecure. Always review generated code and content before you use it, especially in production, security, financial or legal contexts.
  • Credits are deducted only after a successful response.

Reporting harmful or incorrect AI output: if an AI response is offensive, unsafe, misleading or otherwise inappropriate, please tell us at wholeaisupport@gmail.com with a short description, and we will review it.

5. Payments and subscriptions

Paid plans (Pro and Heavy Pro) give you more credits and extra features. How you pay depends on where you use Whole AI:

5.1 Whole AI Android app from Google Play

Purchases made inside the Android app from Google Play are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and billing details under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card number or payment method. We receive only the purchase confirmation (for example, which plan was bought, the purchase token and its status) that we need to activate, renew or end your plan. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the Google Play Store under "Payments & subscriptions".

5.2 Whole AI website and installable web app (outside Google Play)

Paid plans bought on the website or the installable web app are paid in cryptocurrency (USDT on the TRC20 network) and processed through NOWPayments. We create a unique payment for each purchase and store your email, the selected plan, the amount and the transaction details so that we can verify the payment and activate your plan. We do not store your wallet keys, seed phrases or card details. NOWPayments' own terms and privacy policy also apply to that payment.

Plans last for the period shown at purchase and then return to the Free plan unless renewed. Please see the refund terms shown at checkout for your platform.

7. Websites you publish

If you publish a website with Whole AI, the published site is public. Anyone with its link (for example yourname.wholeai.space) or its custom domain can view it. Do not include private or sensitive information in a site you publish. Custom domains are available on paid plans; if a plan expires, visitors may be redirected to your free link, and your site and its data remain until you delete them. You are responsible for the content you publish.

8. Device storage, cookies and caching

  • Local storage: Whole AI keeps some data on your device, such as chat history, feature preferences, sign-in state and a device identifier, so the app works smoothly across sessions. You can clear it from your browser or app settings, but you may be signed out.
  • Service worker cache: the installable web app and Android app cache the home page and app icons so they open faster. API responses, account data, credits and plans are never cached.
  • Cookies: we use only the cookies or similar technologies necessary to run the Service and keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Fraud and abuse prevention

To protect the free daily credits from abuse, one device and one network are allowed to create only one free Whole AI account. To enforce this, we store a device identifier and the IP address together with the email of the account created from that device or network. We use this information only to prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the Service fair for everyone.

10. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our active systems. Some records, such as payment and transaction history, may be kept for as long as needed to meet legal, tax, accounting or fraud-prevention requirements. Backups are removed in the normal course of our backup cycle.

11. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • Inside the app or website: open the menu, go to Profile, and tap Delete My Account. You will be asked to confirm with the same Google account.
  • By email: if you cannot access the app, send a deletion request from your registered email address to wholeaisupport@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Whole AI account". We will verify the request and delete your account and associated data.

When your account is deleted, your profile, plan and credit data, chats and projects are removed from our active systems, subject to the retention exceptions in Section 10. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; please cancel it in Google Play as well.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can:

  • update your profile information from the Profile page at any time;
  • delete your account as described in Section 11;
  • contact us at wholeaisupport@gmail.com for any other request about your data.

We will respond to valid requests within a reasonable time, and as required by applicable law.

13. Security

Your account data is stored using Google Cloud, which applies industry-standard security practices. Data sent between your device and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS. Access to account data is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

14. Children's privacy

Whole AI is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

15. International data transfers

Whole AI and its service providers operate in several countries. Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, which may have different data protection laws. By using the Service, you understand that your information may be transferred in this way, and we rely on our providers' safeguards to protect it.

16. Android app information

The Whole AI Android app is a wrapper that opens the Whole AI web app on your device in a full-screen view, using the same account, the same data and the same practices described in this policy. The app uses internet access to work. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage on its own. Sign-in uses your Google account, and purchases inside the Android app use Google Play Billing.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, our features or the law. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
